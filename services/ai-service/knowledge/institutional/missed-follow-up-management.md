# Missed Follow-up Management Protocol

Synthetic demonstration material. Not validated clinical guidance.

## Purpose

This procedure describes operational handling of a missed follow-up appointment that is already in human review.

## Scope

It applies only to synthetic demonstration operations for a missed follow-up appointment. It does not read a patient record and it does not change a review case.

## Operational Trigger

The deterministic protocol has already required human review for a missed follow-up appointment without a confirmed future follow-up. This procedure does not decide that match.

## Review Procedure

Confirm that human review is already required for the missed follow-up appointment. Check whether a confirmed future follow-up is already recorded by the deterministic protocol. When coordination is still needed, plan follow-up coordination. When no operational action remains, record that the review is completed without operational action.

## Human Authority

A person holds human authority over the operational outcome. The procedure does not assign that authority to a model.

## Outcomes

The named operational outcomes are follow-up coordination planned and review completed without operational action.

## Non-goals

This procedure does not interpret clinical meaning, decide whether an appointment was missed, execute the deterministic protocol, redefine its rules, create a review case, or close a review case.
